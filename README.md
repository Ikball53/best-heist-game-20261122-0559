# COPPER ORRERY — Heist

A clockwork celestial vault holds the Copper Orrery, a brass machine that maps the night sky. You are the thief who slips through five gear-lit floors — Entry Rotunda, Gear Gallery, Astrolabe Hall, Clockwork Crypt, and the Orrery Core — lifting glowing gems, chalices, crowns and orrery rings while patrolling wardens and sweeping brass cameras hunt for you. Clear every treasure on a floor to open its exit, and reach the heart of the machine to win.

Named mechanics: vision cones on guards and cameras, an alarm meter that fills while you are seen and cools in shadow, red laser tripwires that spike the alarm, creaky floorboards that make noise unless you crouch, smoke vials that hide you for a few seconds, tossed coins that pull guards' gaze toward the mouse, and crouching behind crates to break line of sight. Caught means the alarm filled completely.

Controls: WASD or arrows to move, Shift to crouch (slower, silent, hides behind cover), Q for a smoke vial, E to toss a coin at the mouse pointer, M to mute. Sound is generated live with WebAudio — footsteps, loot chimes, creaks, tripwire zaps, alarms and the win fanfare. Open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome; it plays fully offline.
